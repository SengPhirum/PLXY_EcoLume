# Cambodia rollout plan

## Phase 0 — design and approval

- Confirm ministry owner, operators, maintenance parties, data classification, and service levels.
- Survey current luminaires, drivers, power quality, pole identifiers, and carrier coverage.
- Select DALI/0–10 V integration and final LTE bands.
- Complete safety, cybersecurity, procurement, and telecom review.

## Phase 1 — supervised pilot

Deploy 20–50 lights in one Phnom Penh district with varied road and power conditions. Run for at least 8–12 weeks. Validate energy calculations, alert accuracy, heat, surge behavior, GPS, carrier uptime, and technician workflow.

## Phase 2 — representative provinces

Add one urban/tourism province and one rural province. Validate multiple maintenance teams, long travel time, weak coverage, spares, and regional escalation.

## Phase 3 — controlled national rollout

Roll out by regional waves with entry/exit criteria. Maintain asset reconciliation, installation QA sampling, rollback capacity, and weekly incident review.

## Operating model

| Level | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| National operations centre | Availability, security, major incidents, standards, reporting |
| Provincial coordinator | Local asset accuracy, maintenance prioritization, escalation |
| Field technician/vendor | Safe repair, evidence, parts, closure and calibration |
| Security team | Monitoring, access review, vulnerability and incident response |

## Suggested service priorities

| Priority | Example | Target response |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | Dark high-risk junction, exposed electrical hazard | Immediate dispatch |
| High | Multiple adjacent lights offline | Same operational shift |
| Medium | Single lamp failure on normal road | Planned within SLA |
| Low | Weak signal with normal lighting | Next scheduled visit |

Final targets must be approved based on road classification, technician coverage, and contractual support.
